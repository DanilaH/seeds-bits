## Diagnosis
Past fixes solved actual accessibility, routing, safety, persistence and geometry problems. Preserve these. But they optimized every screen as a data container. Hierarchy "one main action" became generic rectangular surfaces, progress meters and small icons; objects became labels of panels. Home: aligned rows like service menu. Notebook/picker: numeric table + filter rather than opening a book. Result: document with checkmarks, summary rows, stats. Progress: hero percent, counters, duplicated totals, stats, further collection navigation. Jar is currently a pictogram supporting a percentage rather than an actual reward. Gameplay: sparse fields can float in empty area between utilitarian strips; dense field needs all usable area, not additional decoration. Theme insufficiently articulated; mostly pale neutral-green UI plus gold button. These are design judgments, not measured live retention.

## Brief
Promise: a quiet, immediately accessible number game, pleasant to return to and satisfying to clear. Pillars: real digits remain readable; recognizable objects and memorable silhouette outside field; one obvious action; visible progress through actual earned things rather than reports. Anti-goals: generic fintech/dashboard panels; mascot/quest/story/currency expansion; artificial locks; fake progress; sweeping restyle of gameplay field or repeating visual experiments in public settings; changing core feel work, economy, generator, ads, all 2496 tasks, assigned pins/Undo, six paused experiments. 
Keep all three mode entries on Home, exact resume and separate quick-start notebook CTA when active!=book, choose difficulty, Other days independent right control, fixed banner rail, Back policies, safe hit targets. No feature deletion by art.
 
## Concepts (proposed, owner chooses)
A — **Quiet tabletop / Тихий стол** (recommended), S1–S2 material metaphor, no mascot. A small editorial/tactile scene, not a 3D room: current open notebook or number puzzle on warm paper with slightly textured, restrained material, visibly real bookmark and one dominant Continue, a small jar as clickable collection destination. Classic number tiles, notebook/bookmark, daily tear-off calendar are distinct objects/entries, not three identical rectangular cards. Secondary mode buttons remain labeled and independent. Notebook is one sheet with all 24 positions in flat 4x6 or 6x4; solved markings are stamps, current bookmark, no heavy per-page sections, no fake locks. Victory: clear space/ink mark/seed arrival and one dominant next action; stats details secondary, reward actually committed. Collection: a shelf of earned jars with distinct physical silhouettes and real filling; all previous stats retained under optional statistics disclosure. One asset family, e.g. 3 mode objects + jar master + paper/stamp, variants via vector/style, not 100 separate illustrations. Keep exact current digits/cell appearance initially. Moderate production cost, risk of excessive skeuomorphism or low contrast.
B — **Graphic puzzle / Графическая игра**, S0/no mascot. A bold number-led visual identity and typographic hierarchy, strong forest ink, cream and concentrated warm accent; oversized brand/match motif and sparse primary action, modes as distinct large labels/glyphs separated by whitespace, no card chrome. Notebook is editorial numbered index, simple marks, victory large typographic clear and restrained seed accent, collection is the one art-rich area. Lower asset cost; risk of looking like a generic minimal app if type/composition weak. 
Neither variant proposes changing actual number puzzle artwork or input. Both are intentionally different compositions, not mere palette variants. No new mechanics, mascots, money or lore.
