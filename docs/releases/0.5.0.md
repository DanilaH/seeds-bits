## Changes

- 100 notebooks, 24 tasks each: 2400 tasks. Original 24 boards, IDs and solutions retained. Last volume has 466–594 digits; task 2400 has 536. Fields scroll vertically without shrinking digits. Volume groups, filters, pages and Continue keep the catalog navigable.
- New content is deterministic generated composition with verified solutions and structural deduplication, including digit renamings. It is loaded lazily and decoded on demand. This is not a claim of individually handcrafted or human-balanced puzzles.
- Daily archive and monthly trophies begin January 2025.
- Classic pairs fill collectible jars; percentage, completed jars, and clear-board milestones persist. Undo/replay cannot credit the same progress twice. Explicit restart starts a new attempt. Earlier untracked play is not retroactively invented.
- Two varied seed flights with trails, landing/fill/full feedback and quiet optional sounds. Animation does not block subsequent moves; backgrounding clears flights, and reduced motion settles immediately. Sound and vibration have independent settings.
- Save format 3 migrates version 2, preserves boards/completion and protects future versions. Damaged optional jar metadata does not discard boards.

## Performance and limits

Same Chromium, 390×844 viewport, 1800-cell board, 15 pairs, three alternating runs against 0.4.1. Final median browser task duration: 3359ms before, 3402ms after (+1.3%). Shared-host measurements are noisy and individual long tasks remain; this does not establish 60 FPS or low-end Android performance.

Earlier canvas and CSS offset-path approaches were replaced by bounded transform/opacity keyframe particles. Jar fill uses clip-path rather than animated height. Intermediate measurements and final data are retained in `0.5.0-evidence/`.

Physical Android install/update, process death, TalkBack, real speaker feel and low-end device performance have not been tested. Automated solution validation does not establish the subjective difficulty of every generated task.
