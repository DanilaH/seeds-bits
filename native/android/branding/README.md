# Launcher artwork

Selected direction, 2026-10-22: ivory 3 and 7 puzzle tiles with a striped sunflower seed, on a teal gradient. Generated with the built-in image generation tool and selected by the owner.

`store/icon-512.png` is the store listing icon. `launcher-art.webp` is the matching splash artwork; `icon-*.png` are Lanczos exports for legacy Android launchers. `adaptive-art.webp` extends the same gradient around a smaller central composition, so Android's circular and rounded masks retain both numbers and the seed. Its full canvas is the adaptive background; the foreground is transparent.

Generation brief: two ivory tactile tiles bearing dark navy 3 and 7, a striped sunflower seed at their lower edge, turquoise blue full-bleed gradient, no slogans or app name. Adaptive edit brief: preserve the design and arrangement; fit the entire subject group within the central 52% of the canvas and extend the gradient to every edge.

Native WebP exports are lossless and decoded RGB pixels equal the retained PNG sources. Store artwork remains PNG.
